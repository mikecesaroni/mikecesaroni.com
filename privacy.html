Privacy Policy

The Round Table LLC, doing business as The Working Class · Effective September 30, 2026

This policy explains what The Round Table LLC, doing business as The Working Class ("we," "us"), collects when you use our website, message us on social media, book a call, or buy one of our programs, and what we do with it. We keep it short on purpose. If anything here is unclear, email us at mikecesaroni1@gmail.com.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out the qualifying quiz, book a call, message us on Instagram or Facebook, use the chat widget, or make a purchase, you may give us your name, email address, phone number, your business's industry and approximate revenue, which of our programs you're interested in, and anything you write to us.

Information collected automatically. Embedded services on this site (the video player, the booking calendars, the chat widget) may set their own cookies and collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed, under their own privacy policies. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number goes to Stripe, not to us. We receive confirmation of the payment and the last four digits of the card.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and route your inquiry to the right next step.
  • To schedule calls and send confirmations and reminders.
  • To deliver the program you purchased, including access to our community.
  • To send you information about our programs when you have asked for it, which you can stop at any time.
  • To keep records of purchases and to meet legal and tax obligations.

Text messaging (SMS)

By providing your mobile number on this site, in a booking form, or in a message to us, you agree to receive calls and text messages from The Round Table LLC about your inquiry, your appointments and our programs. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or email mikecesaroni1@gmail.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use-case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Google user data

This section covers data we access through Google APIs on behalf of our advertising clients. It is separate from the information we collect about visitors to this site. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Ads. We use the Google Ads API under the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords scope. Google does not publish a read-only version of this scope, so this is the only one available, but our application only reads. It never creates, edits, pauses or deletes anything in a Google Ads account.

Only our own team account signs in. Clients never authenticate with Google through our application. We reach a client's Google Ads account because that client has added our team email as a user on their account, or has linked their account to our manager account.

Each night we read: the list of accounts our login can see, per-day campaign performance (impressions, clicks, cost, conversions and conversion value), per-day keyword performance, per-day search terms, and account status. We also read Keyword Planner data, including search volume, competition and bid estimates, when a member of our team builds a keyword plan.

We do this to report each client's advertising results to them, to find spend that is not producing leads, and to plan keywords for their campaigns.

Google Drive. We use the Google Drive API under the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly scope through a service account, not through a sign-in screen. A client shares a folder of photos with that service account so we can use their images in their ads. It is read-only, it cannot see anything a client has not explicitly shared with it, and it cannot change or delete files.

Where it is stored. In our own database, hosted on Amazon Web Services, in tables holding daily campaign, keyword and search-term rows, weekly totals and saved keyword plans. The OAuth refresh token is held as an encrypted server-side secret and is never exposed to a browser.

How long we keep it. For as long as the client is a client. Deleted within 30 days of a client leaving us, or sooner if they ask.

Who sees it. The client, in their own reports, and our team. Aggregated performance figures, never credentials and never personal data, may be sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, when a member of our team asks it to analyse a client's advertising. We do not sell Google user data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or for any advertising or marketing purpose of our own.

What we do not use. Our application does not request or access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Business Profile, Google Analytics or Google Search Console data.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers who run parts of our business on our behalf, and only for that purpose:

  • GoHighLevel (LeadConnector), our customer relationship system, which stores your contact details, sends our emails and texts, hosts our booking calendars and chat widget, and processes purchases.
  • Stripe, which processes payments.
  • Google, for the Google Meet sessions our calls and coaching meetings run on.
  • Skool, which hosts our member community.
  • Meta (Instagram and Facebook), when you message us there.
  • FormSubmit, an email relay that delivers website form submissions to our inbox.
  • YouTube, for the video embedded on our homepage.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we have a business reason to: while you are a lead or a member, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, except where we have to keep a record of a purchase.

Your choices

  • Texts: reply STOP to any message.
  • Emails: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. Transactional emails such as booking confirmations and receipts still go out.
  • Access, correction or deletion: email mikecesaroni1@gmail.com and we will handle it within 30 days.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee against every risk.

Children

Our programs are for business owners. This site is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
